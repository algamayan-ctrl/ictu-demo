Status: FOR REVIEW. This is a collection/verification plan, not completed institutional evaluation.

The read-only manuscript objective3 names ISO/IEC25010:2023 Functional Suitability and Interaction Capability. Objective5 names Time Efficiency, Accuracy and Productivity. The scope paragraph specifies five IT Experts validating modules/process flow before deployment and at least30 comparable process simulations for operational improvement after deployment. The user confirmed following this manuscript sequence: prepare collection tools now and perform real operational KPI measurement after approved deployment. No manuscript or respondent sampling was edited.

## Pre-deployment technical and quality evaluation

| Characteristic | Development evidence | Actual user/expert evidence still required |
|---|---|---|
| Functional Suitability | Correct saved-version affiliation, registration permissions, approval queues, exact reservation, physical return/laundry accounting, cancellation authority, report denominators and failure/rollback tests | Approved functional checklist executed by the specified experts; record observed defects, corrections and retests. Automated test counts are not expert responses |
| Interaction Capability | Actual desktop/mobile layout, readable counts, bounded widths, validation messages, keyboard/date-picker behavior and role navigation | Execute the approved evaluation instrument with authorized evaluators; accessibility/device/task observations and questionnaire results must be recorded truthfully |

Keep test receipts, application revision, dependency versions, database engine, actor/fixture, expected output, actual output and evaluator/time with each item. A screenshot proves the captured screen, not every device or the entire workflow.

## Operational KPI collection

Use the companion empty CSV as a raw collection template. It intentionally contains no invented timings, success rates, staff effort, targets or improvement percentages. Confirm the supplied standalone KPI definitions/targets when the missing attachment becomes available.

| KPI | Manuscript meaning | Proposed measurement detail — requires approved baseline/protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Time Efficiency | Reduction in process handling time | Compare equivalent manually recorded start/end handling times for the same process and scope. Record waiting time separately; an approval queue's elapsed duration is not automatically staff handling time |
| Accuracy | Record/transaction correctness and reduction in operational errors | Record an independent inspection/check result and the inspected population. Define what counts as an error before collection; compare equivalent datasets |
| Productivity | Completed process output relative to staff effort | Record completed work and actual staff effort under comparable scope/conditions. Do not treat request volume or a scheduled booking as completed output |

Preserve case matching, process type, period, measurement units, source evidence, staff effort, correctness checks and exclusions. Require at least30 comparable process simulations as specified by the manuscript when the approved evaluation stage is reached. Technical fixtures, automated test repetitions and synthetic recovery records are excluded from real operational KPI results.

Targets, approved baseline, evaluator identity, sign-off, operational observation data and institutional instrument remain unverified. Blank/missing denominators must produce “Not measured”, never a fabricated0% or100%. Keep quality questionnaires separate from operational KPI records and reports.
